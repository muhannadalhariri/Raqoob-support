# Raqoob privacy policy / سياسة خصوصية راقوب

Effective 21 October 2026.

Raqoob reads performance and power information on your Mac to display it locally. The app does not require an account, use analytics or advertising SDKs, or send these readings to us. It does not collect personal data from the app. It stores your language choice on your Mac using macOS preferences.

If you choose to contact us through GitHub Issues, the information you submit there is handled by GitHub under its own privacy terms. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue.

We may update this policy if the app's data practices change. The current version will remain at this URL. For privacy questions, open a support issue without including private details and request a private contact route.
